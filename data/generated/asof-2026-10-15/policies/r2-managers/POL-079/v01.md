# Probation Review Process
Effective from 2026-09-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
The Probation Review Process policy sets the standard rules that people managers follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A probation review must be completed within 87 calendar days.
- Probation coaching requires 54 hours.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-419.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Learning and Development team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Learning and Development team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
