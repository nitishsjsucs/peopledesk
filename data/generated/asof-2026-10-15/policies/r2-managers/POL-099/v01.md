# Performance Improvement Plans
Effective from 2026-04-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This document is the Performance Improvement Plans policy. It explains what people managers can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A performance improvement plan must be completed within 81 calendar days.
- Improvement plan coaching requires 64 hours.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-412.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Talent Management team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Talent Management team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
