# Team Coverage Planning
Effective from 2026-05-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Team Coverage Planning works for people managers and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A coverage plan for extended leave must be completed within 109 calendar days.
- Coverage handover documentation requires 79 hours.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-434.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the People Operations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the People Operations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
