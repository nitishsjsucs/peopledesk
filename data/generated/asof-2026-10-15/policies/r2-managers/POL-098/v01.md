# Calibration Guidelines
Effective from 2024-07-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This document is the Calibration Guidelines policy. It explains what people managers can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Calibration session preparation requires 42 hours.
- Calibration rating changes must be completed within 101 calendar days.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-420.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Talent Management team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Talent Management team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
