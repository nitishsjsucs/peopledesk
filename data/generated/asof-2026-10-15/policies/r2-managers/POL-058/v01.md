# Quarterly Access Reviews
Effective from 2026-02-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
The Quarterly Access Reviews policy sets the standard rules that people managers follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A quarterly access review must be completed within 118 calendar days.
- Access review preparation per quarter requires 68 hours.
- Policy owners review this section every 6 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-420.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the IT Security team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the IT Security team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
