# Benefits Escalation for Managers
Effective from 2025-10-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This document is the Benefits Escalation for Managers policy. It explains what people managers can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Escalation of an unresolved benefits case must be completed within 68 calendar days.
- The maximum benefits exception budget per team is $4,050.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-483.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Total Rewards team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Total Rewards team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
