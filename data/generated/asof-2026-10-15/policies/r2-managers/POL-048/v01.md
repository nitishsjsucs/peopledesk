# Remote Team Check-ins
Effective from 2025-02-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
The Remote Team Check-ins policy sets the standard rules that people managers follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum in-person team gathering budget is $3,050.
- Remote team check-in time per quarter requires 79 hours.
- Policy owners review this section every 24 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-430.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Experience team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Experience team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
