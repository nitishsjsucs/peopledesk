# Handling Misconduct Reports
Effective from 2026-01-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
The Handling Misconduct Reports policy sets the standard rules that people managers follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Escalation of a misconduct report must be completed within 120 calendar days.
- Misconduct intake training for managers requires 72 hours.
- Policy owners review this section every 24 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-468.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Employee Relations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Employee Relations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
