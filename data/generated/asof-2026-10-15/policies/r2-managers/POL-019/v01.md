# Return-to-Work Accommodations
Effective from 2026-09-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Return-to-Work Accommodations works for people managers and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A return-to-work accommodation plan must be completed within 85 calendar days.
- The maximum accommodation equipment budget is $2,250.
- Return-to-work transition support requires 73 hours.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-485.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Total Rewards team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Total Rewards team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
