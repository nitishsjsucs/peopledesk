# Safety Inspections for Managers
Effective from 2026-10-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This document is the Safety Inspections for Managers policy. It explains what people managers can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Workspace safety inspection requires 74 hours.
- Fixing an inspection finding must be completed within 86 calendar days.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-478.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Safety team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Safety team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
