# Hybrid Exception Approvals
Effective from 2026-01-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Hybrid Exception Approvals works for people managers and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A decision on a hybrid exception must be completed within 86 calendar days.
- The maximum home office upgrade for an approved exception is $4,550.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-402.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Experience team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Experience team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
