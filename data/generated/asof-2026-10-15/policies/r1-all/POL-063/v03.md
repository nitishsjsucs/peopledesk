# Conflicts of Interest
Effective from 2026-07-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Conflicts of Interest policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Conflict of interest disclosure must be completed within 25 calendar days.
- The maximum outside business investment without disclosure is $1,775.
- Policy owners review this section every 24 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-492.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Employee Relations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Employee Relations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
