# Career Levels Overview
Effective from 2025-12-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Career Levels Overview policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Annual career development planning requires 40 hours.
- Career conversation with your manager is limited to 1 time per calendar year.
- Policy owners review this section every 24 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-451.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Talent Management team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Talent Management team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
