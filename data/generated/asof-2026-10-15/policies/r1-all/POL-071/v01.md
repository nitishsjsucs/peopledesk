# New Hire Onboarding Checklist
Effective from 2026-09-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
The New Hire Onboarding Checklist policy sets the standard rules that all employees follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The new hire onboarding checklist must be completed within 38 calendar days.
- Self-paced onboarding modules requires 24 hours.
- Policy owners review this section every 24 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-491.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Learning and Development team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Learning and Development team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
