# Employee Assistance Program
Effective from 2026-10-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Employee Assistance Program policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Free counseling session is limited to 2 times per calendar year.
- Counselor assignment after a request must be completed within 6 calendar days.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-413.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Total Rewards team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Total Rewards team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
