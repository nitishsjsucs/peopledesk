# Mandatory Compliance Training
Effective from 2026-02-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Mandatory Compliance Training policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Mandatory compliance training requires 3 hours.
- Mandatory compliance training completion must be completed within 26 calendar days.
- Policy owners review this section every 6 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-460.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Learning and Development team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Learning and Development team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
