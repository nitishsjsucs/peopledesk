# Health Insurance Enrollment
Effective from 2026-08-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Health Insurance Enrollment policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Health plan coverage begins after a waiting period of 55 days.
- Health plan enrollment must be completed within 38 calendar days.
- Plan change outside open enrollment is limited to 2 times per calendar year.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-426.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Total Rewards team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Total Rewards team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
