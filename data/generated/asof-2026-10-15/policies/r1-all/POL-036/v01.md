# Corporate Card Use
Effective from 2025-10-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
The Corporate Card Use policy sets the standard rules that all employees follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Single card purchases above $1,200 require written pre-approval.
- The maximum daily corporate card spend is $250.
- Corporate card statement reconciliation must be completed within 48 calendar days.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-498.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Finance team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Finance team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
