# Buddy Program
Effective from 2025-08-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Buddy Program policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The buddy program time commitment requires 11 hours.
- Buddy assignment must be completed within 50 calendar days.
- Buddy lunch reimbursement is limited to 5 times per calendar year.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-461.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Learning and Development team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Learning and Development team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
