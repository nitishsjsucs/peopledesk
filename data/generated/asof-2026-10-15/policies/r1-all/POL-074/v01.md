# Learning Budget
Effective from 2026-05-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
The Learning Budget policy sets the standard rules that all employees follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum annual learning budget is $600.
- Conference attendance is limited to 9 times per calendar year.
- Learning budget course approval must be completed within 17 calendar days.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-430.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Learning and Development team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Learning and Development team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
