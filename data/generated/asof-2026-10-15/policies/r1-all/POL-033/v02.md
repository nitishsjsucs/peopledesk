# Lodging Limits
Effective from 2026-09-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Lodging Limits policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum nightly hotel rate is $1,500.
- Hotel cancellation must be completed within 49 calendar days.
- Hotel stay above the nightly rate is limited to 7 times per calendar year.
- Policy owners review this section every 24 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-494.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Finance team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Finance team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
