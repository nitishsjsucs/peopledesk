# Overtime Eligibility
Effective from 2025-11-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Overtime Eligibility policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Overtime eligibility in a work week requires 11 hours.
- The overtime premium is 9% of eligible pay.
- Weekend overtime shift is limited to 8 times per calendar year.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-496.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Payroll and Compensation team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Payroll and Compensation team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
