# Gifts and Hospitality
Effective from 2025-12-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
The Gifts and Hospitality policy sets the standard rules that all employees follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum value of a gift from a vendor is $1,800.
- Vendor hospitality acceptance is limited to 6 times per calendar year.
- Gift declaration must be completed within 13 calendar days.
- Policy owners review this section every 24 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-485.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Employee Relations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Employee Relations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
