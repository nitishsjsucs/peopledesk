# Incident Reporting
Effective from 2026-04-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Incident Reporting policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Incident reporting must be completed within 13 calendar days.
- Near-miss report review requires 18 hours.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-442.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Safety team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Safety team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
