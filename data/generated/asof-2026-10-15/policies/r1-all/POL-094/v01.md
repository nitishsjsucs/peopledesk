# Feedback Guidelines
Effective from 2026-02-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Feedback Guidelines works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Peer feedback request is limited to 4 times per calendar year.
- Written feedback after a project ends must be completed within 31 calendar days.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-406.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Talent Management team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Talent Management team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
