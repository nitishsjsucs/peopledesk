# Mileage Reimbursement
Effective from 2024-01-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
The Mileage Reimbursement policy sets the standard rules that all employees follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Mileage claim submission must be completed within 41 calendar days.
- The maximum monthly mileage reimbursement is $1,675.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-446.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Finance team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Finance team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
