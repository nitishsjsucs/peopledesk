# Social Media Use
Effective from 2026-07-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
The Social Media Use policy sets the standard rules that all employees follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Social media guidelines training requires 37 hours.
- Removal of a flagged social media post must be completed within 23 calendar days.
- Policy owners review this section every 6 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-431.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Employee Relations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Employee Relations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
