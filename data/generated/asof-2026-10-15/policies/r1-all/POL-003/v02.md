# Parental Leave
Effective from 2026-02-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
The Parental Leave policy sets the standard rules that all employees follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A parental leave request requires 4 weeks of notice.
- Birth certificate filing for parental leave must be completed within 34 calendar days.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-470.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the People Operations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the People Operations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
