# Remote Work Tax Compliance
Effective from 2026-02-01. Applies to: HR administrators.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Remote Work Tax Compliance works for HR administrators and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to HR administrators in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A remote work tax review must be completed within 164 calendar days.
- The maximum cross-border tax advisory fee is $5,575.
- Policy owners review this section every 12 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-487.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Experience team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Experience team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
