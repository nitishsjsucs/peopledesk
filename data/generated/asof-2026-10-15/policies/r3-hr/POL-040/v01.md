# Expense Fraud Review
Effective from 2025-11-01. Applies to: HR administrators.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Expense Fraud Review works for HR administrators and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to HR administrators in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- An expense fraud review must be completed within 170 calendar days.
- Flagged expense reimbursements above $9,675 require written pre-approval.
- The expense audit sampling rate is 69% of eligible pay.
- Policy owners review this section every 6 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-473.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Finance team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Finance team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
