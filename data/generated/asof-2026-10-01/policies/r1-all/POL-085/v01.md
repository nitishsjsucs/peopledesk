# First Aid Coverage
Effective from 2026-01-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the First Aid Coverage policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- First aid certification training requires 26 hours.
- First aid kit inspection is limited to 1 time per calendar year.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-489.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Safety team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Safety team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
