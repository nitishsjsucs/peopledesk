# Password and MFA
Effective from 2025-10-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Password and MFA policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Password rotation must be completed within 22 calendar days.
- Multi-factor device reset is limited to 8 times per calendar year.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-467.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the IT Security team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the IT Security team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
