# Self-Assessment
Effective from 2025-04-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Self-Assessment policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Self-assessment completion must be completed within 40 calendar days.
- Self-assessment preparation requires 14 hours.
- Policy owners review this section every 12 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-440.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Talent Management team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Talent Management team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
