# Lost Device Reporting
Effective from 2027-01-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Lost Device Reporting works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Lost device reporting must be completed within 12 calendar days.
- The maximum replacement device deductible is $1,500.
- Policy owners review this section every 12 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-448.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the IT Security team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the IT Security team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
