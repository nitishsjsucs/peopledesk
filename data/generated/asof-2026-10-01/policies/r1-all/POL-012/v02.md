# Dental and Vision Coverage
Effective from 2026-05-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Dental and Vision Coverage works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum annual vision frame allowance is $1,850.
- Covered dental cleaning visit is limited to 5 times per calendar year.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-435.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Total Rewards team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Total Rewards team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
