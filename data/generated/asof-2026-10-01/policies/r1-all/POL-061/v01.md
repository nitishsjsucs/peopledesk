# Code of Conduct
Effective from 2023-08-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
The Code of Conduct policy sets the standard rules that all employees follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Code of conduct training requires 10 hours.
- Code of conduct attestation must be completed within 47 calendar days.
- Conduct policy acknowledgment is limited to 7 times per calendar year.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-401.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Employee Relations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Employee Relations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
