# Coworking Space Access
Effective from 2025-05-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Coworking Space Access works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Coworking day pass use is limited to 8 times per calendar year.
- The maximum coworking membership reimbursement is $825.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-490.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Experience team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Experience team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
