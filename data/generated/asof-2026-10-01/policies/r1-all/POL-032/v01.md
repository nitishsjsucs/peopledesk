# Meal Per Diem
Effective from 2026-03-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Meal Per Diem policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum daily meal allowance on business trips is $900.
- A trip that qualifies for the meal allowance requires 30 hours.
- Policy owners review this section every 12 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-471.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Finance team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Finance team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
