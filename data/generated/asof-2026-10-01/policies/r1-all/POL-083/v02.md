# Ergonomics Assessment
Effective from 2025-12-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Ergonomics Assessment works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum ergonomic equipment allowance is $1,525.
- Ergonomics assessment is limited to 1 time per calendar year.
- Ergonomic assessment scheduling must be completed within 24 calendar days.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-418.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Safety team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Safety team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
