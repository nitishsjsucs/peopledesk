# Internet Reimbursement
Effective from 2025-09-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
The Internet Reimbursement policy sets the standard rules that all employees follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum monthly internet reimbursement is $1,625.
- Internet bill upload must be completed within 17 calendar days.
- Internet speed upgrade is limited to 8 times per calendar year.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-445.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Experience team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Experience team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
