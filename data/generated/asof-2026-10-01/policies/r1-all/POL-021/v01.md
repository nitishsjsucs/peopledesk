# Payroll Schedule
Effective from 2026-05-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Payroll Schedule works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Timesheet approval before payroll must be completed within 35 calendar days.
- Off-cycle payroll run is limited to 10 times per calendar year.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-462.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Payroll and Compensation team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Payroll and Compensation team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
