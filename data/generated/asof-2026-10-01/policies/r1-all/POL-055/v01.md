# Software Installation Requests
Effective from 2026-07-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Software Installation Requests policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Security review of a software request must be completed within 21 calendar days.
- The maximum self-service software purchase is $325.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-400.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the IT Security team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the IT Security team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
