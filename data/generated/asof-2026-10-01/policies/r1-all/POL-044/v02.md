# Working From Another Country
Effective from 2026-05-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Working From Another Country policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A single work-from-abroad stay must be completed within 6 calendar days.
- A work-from-abroad request requires 12 weeks of notice.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-485.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Experience team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Experience team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
