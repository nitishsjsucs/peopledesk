# Public Holidays
Effective from 2026-03-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
The Public Holidays policy sets the standard rules that all employees follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Floating holiday use is limited to 6 times per calendar year.
- A floating holiday request requires 2 weeks of notice.
- Policy owners review this section every 6 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-411.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the People Operations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the People Operations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
