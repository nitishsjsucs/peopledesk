# Sick Leave
Effective from 2026-02-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Sick Leave works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Sick leave accrues at 2.0 days per month.
- Sick note delivery must be completed within 33 calendar days.
- Sick leave without a doctor's note is limited to 10 times per calendar year.
- Policy owners review this section every 6 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-424.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the People Operations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the People Operations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
