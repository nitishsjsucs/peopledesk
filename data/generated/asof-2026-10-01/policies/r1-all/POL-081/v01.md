# Workplace Safety
Effective from 2025-07-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Workplace Safety works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Workplace safety training requires 28 hours.
- Fire drill is limited to 7 times per calendar year.
- Hazard report follow-up must be completed within 34 calendar days.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-446.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Safety team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Safety team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
