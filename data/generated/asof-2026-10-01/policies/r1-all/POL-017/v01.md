# Life Insurance
Effective from 2024-06-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Life Insurance works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Life insurance coverage begins after a waiting period of 25 days.
- The maximum supplemental life insurance premium subsidy is $425.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-463.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Total Rewards team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Total Rewards team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
