# Retirement Plan Match
Effective from 2024-09-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Retirement Plan Match works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The retirement plan match is 13.5% of eligible pay.
- Retirement plan matching begins after a waiting period of 17 days.
- A retirement contribution change must be completed within 57 calendar days.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-414.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Total Rewards team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Total Rewards team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
