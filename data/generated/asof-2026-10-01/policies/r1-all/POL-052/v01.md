# Device Encryption
Effective from 2026-09-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Device Encryption policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Disk encryption on a new laptop must be completed within 43 calendar days.
- Encryption key recovery request is limited to 10 times per calendar year.
- Policy owners review this section every 24 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-495.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the IT Security team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the IT Security team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
