# Equipment Return
Effective from 2025-06-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Equipment Return works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Equipment return after separation must be completed within 43 calendar days.
- The maximum charge for unreturned equipment is $1,800.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-400.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Experience team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Experience team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
