# Business Travel Booking
Effective from 2025-12-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
The Business Travel Booking policy sets the standard rules that all employees follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Airfare bookings above $1,250 require written pre-approval.
- Booking international travel requires 8 weeks of notice.
- The maximum airline seat upgrade reimbursement is $975.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-497.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Finance team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Finance team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
