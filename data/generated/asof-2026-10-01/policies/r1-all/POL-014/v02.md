# Wellness Stipend
Effective from 2026-06-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Wellness Stipend policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum annual wellness stipend is $275.
- Wellness stipend reimbursement request is limited to 7 times per calendar year.
- Wellness receipt upload must be completed within 53 calendar days.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-469.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Total Rewards team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Total Rewards team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
