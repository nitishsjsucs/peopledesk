# Expense Report Deadlines
Effective from 2025-12-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Expense Report Deadlines policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Expense report submission must be completed within 38 calendar days.
- Late expense report exception is limited to 12 times per calendar year.
- The maximum receipt-free expense is $500.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-432.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Finance team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Finance team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
