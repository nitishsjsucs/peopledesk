# Commuter Benefits
Effective from 2026-03-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Commuter Benefits works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum monthly transit pass subsidy is $1,925.
- A commuter benefit election change must be completed within 10 calendar days.
- Parking permit replacement is limited to 4 times per calendar year.
- Policy owners review this section every 6 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-455.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Total Rewards team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Total Rewards team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
