# Tuition Assistance
Effective from 2025-07-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Tuition Assistance works for all employees and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum annual tuition assistance is $900.
- Tuition reimbursement claim submission must be completed within 8 calendar days.
- A tuition assistance application requires 1 week of notice.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-482.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Learning and Development team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Learning and Development team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
