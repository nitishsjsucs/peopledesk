# Hybrid Work Schedule
Effective from 2026-09-01. Applies to: all employees.
## Purpose
This document is the Hybrid Work Schedule policy. It explains what all employees can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Weekly in-office presence requires 32 hours.
- A change to your hybrid schedule requires 1 week of notice.
- Fully remote week is limited to 4 times per calendar year.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-447.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Experience team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Experience team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
