# Investigation Case Management
Effective from 2026-01-01. Applies to: HR administrators.
## Purpose
The Investigation Case Management policy sets the standard rules that HR administrators follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to HR administrators in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Closing an investigation case must be completed within 137 calendar days.
- Investigation case file review requires 115 hours.
- The maximum external investigator engagement is $8,175.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-415.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Employee Relations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Employee Relations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
