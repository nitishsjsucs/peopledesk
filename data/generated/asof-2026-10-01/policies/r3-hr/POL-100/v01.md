# Promotion Budget Allocation
Effective from 2026-09-01. Applies to: HR administrators.
## Purpose
This document is the Promotion Budget Allocation policy. It explains what HR administrators can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to HR administrators in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The promotion budget is 66% of eligible pay.
- Promotion budget reconciliation must be completed within 147 calendar days.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-499.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Talent Management team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Talent Management team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
