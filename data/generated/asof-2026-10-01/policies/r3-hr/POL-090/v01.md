# Workers Compensation Claims
Effective from 2026-04-01. Applies to: HR administrators.
## Purpose
This document is the Workers Compensation Claims policy. It explains what HR administrators can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to HR administrators in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Filing a workers compensation report with the insurer must be completed within 135 calendar days.
- The workers compensation wage replacement is 71% of eligible pay.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-480.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Safety team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Safety team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
