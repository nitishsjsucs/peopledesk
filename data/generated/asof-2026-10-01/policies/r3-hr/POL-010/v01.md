# Leave Investigation Procedures
Effective from 2026-09-01. Applies to: HR administrators.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Leave Investigation Procedures works for HR administrators and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to HR administrators in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A leave abuse investigation must be completed within 160 calendar days.
- Investigator interview preparation requires 84 hours.
- Policy owners review this section every 12 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-461.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the People Operations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the People Operations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
