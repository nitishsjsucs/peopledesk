# Salary Band Administration
Effective from 2025-08-01. Applies to: HR administrators.
## Purpose
This document is the Salary Band Administration policy. It explains what HR administrators can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to HR administrators in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The salary band spread is 61% of eligible pay.
- A salary band review must be completed within 170 calendar days.
- The maximum salary band exception adjustment is $5,875.
- Policy owners review this section every 6 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-407.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Payroll and Compensation team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Payroll and Compensation team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
