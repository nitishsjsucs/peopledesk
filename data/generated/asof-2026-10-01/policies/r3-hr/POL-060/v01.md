# Insider Risk Investigations
Effective from 2026-02-01. Applies to: HR administrators.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Insider Risk Investigations works for HR administrators and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to HR administrators in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- An insider risk investigation must be completed within 164 calendar days.
- Insider risk case documentation requires 108 hours.
- The maximum forensic analysis budget per case is $7,775.
- Policy owners review this section every 24 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-441.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the IT Security team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the IT Security team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
