# Background Check Procedures
Effective from 2026-04-01. Applies to: HR administrators.
## Purpose
The Background Check Procedures policy sets the standard rules that HR administrators follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to HR administrators in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A background check must be completed within 126 calendar days.
- The maximum background check vendor fee is $6,875.
- The background check rescreening rate is 66% of eligible pay.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-492.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Learning and Development team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Learning and Development team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
