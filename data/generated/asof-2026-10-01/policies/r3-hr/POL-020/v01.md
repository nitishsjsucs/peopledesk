# Benefits Vendor Audit
Effective from 2026-10-01. Applies to: HR administrators.
## Purpose
The Benefits Vendor Audit policy sets the standard rules that HR administrators follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to HR administrators in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A benefits vendor audit must be completed within 129 calendar days.
- The vendor sampling rate is 56% of eligible pay.
- Policy owners review this section every 24 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-405.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Total Rewards team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Total Rewards team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
