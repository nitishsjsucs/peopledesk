# Travel Pre-Approval Thresholds
Effective from 2026-05-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This document is the Travel Pre-Approval Thresholds policy. It explains what people managers can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Team offsite bookings above $4,650 require written pre-approval.
- Manager sign-off on a travel request must be completed within 94 calendar days.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-400.
- Your manager is notified automatically when a request is filed.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Finance team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Finance team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
