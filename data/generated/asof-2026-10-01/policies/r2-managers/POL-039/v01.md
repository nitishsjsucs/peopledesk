# Expense Approval Duties
Effective from 2026-05-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
The Expense Approval Duties policy sets the standard rules that people managers follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Manager expense approval must be completed within 109 calendar days.
- Team expense reports above $2,250 require written pre-approval.
- Expense approver training requires 68 hours.
- Policy owners review this section every 12 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-429.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Finance team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Finance team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
