# Spot Bonus Approval
Effective from 2026-10-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Spot Bonus Approval works for people managers and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum spot bonus per award is $3,050.
- Spot bonus batches above $2,350 require written pre-approval.
- Spot bonus payout must be completed within 72 calendar days.
- Policy owners review this section every 12 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-472.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Payroll and Compensation team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Payroll and Compensation team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
