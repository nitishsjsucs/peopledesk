# Manager Onboarding Responsibilities
Effective from 2026-02-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This document is the Manager Onboarding Responsibilities policy. It explains what people managers can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A new hire's first manager check-in must be completed within 73 calendar days.
- Manager preparation for a new hire requires 52 hours.
- Policy owners review this section every 18 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-486.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Learning and Development team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Learning and Development team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
