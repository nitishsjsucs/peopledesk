# Leave Approval Standards
Effective from 2026-04-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This document is the Leave Approval Standards policy. It explains what people managers can expect and what they must do.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Manager review of a leave request must be completed within 88 calendar days.
- Leave approver training requires 62 hours.
- The maximum leave buyback approval limit is $4,150.
- Policy owners review this section every 24 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-419.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the People Operations team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the People Operations team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
