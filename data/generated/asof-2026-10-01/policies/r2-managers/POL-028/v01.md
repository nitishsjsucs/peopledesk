# Merit Increase Guidelines
Effective from 2025-10-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
The Merit Increase Guidelines policy sets the standard rules that people managers follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- The maximum merit increase is 44% of eligible pay.
- A merit recommendation must be completed within 96 calendar days.
- Policy owners review this section every 12 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-495.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Payroll and Compensation team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Payroll and Compensation team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
