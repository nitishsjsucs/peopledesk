# Offboarding Access Removal
Effective from 2026-01-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
The Offboarding Access Removal policy sets the standard rules that people managers follow, so decisions are consistent across teams.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- Manager confirmation of access removal must be completed within 99 calendar days.
- An offboarding access audit requires 52 hours.
- Policy owners review this section every 6 months.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-412.
- Keep a copy of every approval with the request.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the IT Security team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the IT Security team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
