# Incident Investigation Duties
Effective from 2026-07-01. Applies to: people managers.
## Purpose
This policy describes how Incident Investigation Duties works for people managers and who to ask when something is unclear.
## Scope
This policy applies to people managers in every region where the company operates. Where local law sets a stricter standard, the stricter standard applies.
## Policy
- A manager incident investigation must be completed within 83 calendar days.
- Incident investigation training requires 74 hours.
## Procedure
- File requests through the HR portal using Form PD-461.
- Requests filed late are reviewed case by case.
## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Workplace Safety team and are recorded with the request.
## Contacts
Questions about this policy go to the Workplace Safety team. Open a support request for anything that needs follow-up.
